---
title: QR event tickets and check-in
description: Email a QR ticket to everyone who registers through Google Forms, then let your staff check guests in and out from their phones, without accounts or a scanner app.
---

In this tutorial, each person who registers through your Google Form receives a QR ticket by email. At the door, your staff scan the ticket with their phone camera, or open the ticket link, to check guests in and out.

You need: a registration form with an email question, a [connected SMTP](/guides/smtp-setup/), and the staff added as [team members](/guides/team-management/). All of this works on the Free plan.

## 1. Prepare the form

- Add a question that collects the guest's email address.
- Optional: in Google Forms **Settings**, turn on **Limit to 1 response** so each person gets only one ticket.

## 2. Send the ticket to guests

1. [Create a template](/guides/template-customization/) for the confirmation email. Include `{QR Code}`, and `{Verify Link}` for guests who cannot display images. For example:

   ```text
   Hi {Full name},

   You are registered for {Form title}. Show this QR code at the entrance:

   {QR Code}

   Can't see the code? Use this link: {Verify Link}

   {Unsubscribe link}
   ```

2. [Create a rule](/guides/conditional-logic/) of type **Always Notify** that sends this template to the guest's email field.

Each submission gets its own ticket with a reference code.

## 3. Give your staff a scanner link

1. Create a second template for staff containing `{Check-in Scanner}`, `{Check-out Scanner}` or `{Full Scanner}` (both directions).
2. Send it to your staff by adding them under *Internal Recipients (CC)* in a rule.
3. Each staff member opens the link **once, in the browser they will use at the door**. That browser is then authorized for this form for 7 days. No account or password is needed.

:::caution[Use the same browser]
The authorization is stored in the browser that opened the link. If a staff member opens it inside their email app and later scans with Chrome, Chrome is not authorized. Copy the link into the browser they will scan with.
:::

## 4. Check guests in

Staff can either:

- **Scan** the guest's QR code with the phone camera. It opens the ticket page in the browser.
- **Open** the guest's ticket link, for example if the guest forwards the email.

The ticket page shows whether the ticket is valid, the guest's name, the reference code and the current status. An authorized staff browser also shows **Check In** and **Check Out** buttons.

- A guest who is already checked in cannot be checked in twice.
- Check-out is only possible after check-in.
- Each change is kept for 30 days after the last scan.

People without an authorized browser can still open a ticket to see whether it is valid, but cannot change its status.

## 5. Automatic receipts

After every check-in or check-out, two emails are sent:

- The **guest** receives a confirmation with the new status, the time and the location of the scan when available.
- The **staff member** who scanned receives a record of the scan, with a copy to the form admin.
