This step-by-step implementation guide details how Organizations can set up direct self-service attendance for employees on their office computers using click-to-verify links without requiring physical QR codes or camera scanners, as well as configuring an **Office PC Kiosk Station** using **FormMail Hub**.

By combining Google Forms registration, Team Member authorization, Custom SMTP delivery, and direct verification links (`{Verify Link}`) with browser-based staff authorization cookies, you can turn any office PC or front-desk workstation into an automated clock-in/out station—without purchasing proprietary hardware or installing separate software.

To test form submission triggers before rolling out your office station, explore our interactive [Live Demo Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSc2lkYREd5ePz521uYfBDeumOOoPKeBP87i1aSpwokHdFMIHw/viewform) or install the add-on directly via the [Google Workspace Marketplace Listing](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/formmail_hub/409227874327).

## 2. Step-by-Step Implementation: Office PC Kiosk & Automated Self-Attendance

Follow these 7 implementation steps to deploy camera-free self-attendance on employee office PCs:

### Step 1: Configure Google Form & Add Team Members
1. Open Google Forms™ and create the employee registration/attendance form.
2. In **Settings > Responses**, enable **\"Limit to 1 response\"** (ensures each employee holds a unique reference ticket code).
3. Connect the form to FormMail Hub via the Google Forms™ entry add-on or Google Sheets™ add-on.
4. In the FormMail Hub UI, add all participating employees as **Team Members**.
   > **Note on Permissions:** Only the first user among the form's editors who connects that form to FormMail Hub is granted exclusive Admin rights. Team members have **NO** edit or configuration permissions.

### Step 2: Configure Custom SMTP
1. In the FormMail Hub UI, navigate to **Settings > SMTP Config**.
2. Configure and test your Custom SMTP settings (e.g., **Gmail™ App Password**, **Amazon SES**, **SendGrid**, or corporate relay).
   > **Important Prerequisite:** Admins **MUST** configure and test custom SMTP settings before unlocking Templates, Rules, Campaigns, or Analytics. Until custom SMTP is active, these feature tabs remain strictly locked in the UI.

### Step 3: Create Self-Attendance Template (Must Be Created First)
1. Go to **Email Templates** > **+ Create New Template**.
   > **Template Rule:** For direct office PC self-attendance, do **NOT** insert `{QR Code}`; insert **ONLY** `{Verify Link}` and `{Full Scanner}`.
2. Build the email body with the mandatory dynamic tags:
   - `{Full Scanner}`: Grants check-in/check-out permissions to the employee's office browser.
   - `{Verify Link}`: Direct link used by the employee to trigger Check-in and Check-out actions.
   - `{Form title}`: Displays the form title.
   - `{Unsubscribe Link}`: Form-specific opt-out link.

### Step 4: Configure Email Matching Rule
1. Go to **Rules** > Create an Auto-responder Rule triggered on form submission.
2. Set condition: When a form submission occurs, **IF submitter email matches Team Member email**, route and send the template created in Step 3 to that Team Member.
   *(Note: There is no template for general submitters. Only matching Team Members receive the email containing both `{Full Scanner}` and `{Verify Link}`).*

### Step 5: Employee Office PC Authorization
1. Employee receives the notification email on their office computer.
2. Employee clicks the `{Full Scanner}` link directly inside the email on their main browser to authorize their device.
3. System sets an authorization cookie valid for **7 days** for convenient, seamless daily attendance.
   > **Browser Session Isolation Note:** Because authorization relies on a secure cookie set during link activation, opening the link in one browser (e.g., in-app email viewer) will **NOT** authorize a different browser. Staff must copy and paste the Auth link directly into the exact browser application used for verification.

### Step 6: Daily Attendance Execution Workflow
1. On working days, employee opens their office PC browser.
2. **Arrival:** Employee clicks `{Verify Link}` in their email (or saved bookmark) to perform Check-in.
3. **Departure:** Employee clicks `{Verify Link}` prior to leaving to perform Check-out.
4. Backend updates ticket state (`checkin:{form_id}:{refCode}`) and refreshes Redis TTL to **30 days (2,592,000 seconds)**.

### Step 7: Automated Dual Audit Receipts
Upon every check-in or check-out click:
- **Employee Receipt:** Dispatched immediately confirming status change (`IN`/`OUT`) with UTC timestamp and ticket reference code.
- **Admin Audit Trail:** Dispatched immediately to the Team Member / employee and **CC'd to Form Admin** for compliance monitoring.

## 3. Atomic State Concurrency & 30-Day TTL Rules

The attendance backend guarantees data integrity through strict state transition checks:

| Action | Current Ticket State | Engine Behavior | Key TTL Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Check-in** | `OUT` or Not Scanned | Updates status to `IN`, logs operator email & timestamp. | Refreshes to 30 Days (2,592,000s) |
| **Check-in** | `IN` | **Rejected with `409 Conflict`** (prevents duplicate clock-ins). | Unchanged (keeps existing state) |
| **Check-out** | `IN` | Validates active status, updates status to `OUT`, logs operator & timestamp. | Refreshes to 30 Days (2,592,000s) |
| **Check-out** | `OUT` or Not Scanned | Denied (ticket must be checked in first). | Unchanged |

## 4. Troubleshooting & Operational FAQ

| Issue / Symptom | Root Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **\"Feature Locked\" message in sidebar** | Custom SMTP not yet tested or verified. | Admins MUST configure and test Custom SMTP under **Settings > SMTP Config** to unlock Templates, Rules, Campaigns, and Analytics. |
| **Office PC shows \"Unauthorized Scanner\"** | Auth cookie missing or opened in a different browser. | Copy the `{Full Scanner}` link from the email and paste it directly into the office PC's primary browser address bar (authorizes for 7 days). |
| **Employee registered multiple times** | \"Limit to 1 response\" disabled in Google Forms. | Turn ON \"Limit to 1 response\" in Google Forms Settings > Responses. The backend uses `checkin:{form_id}:{refCode}` as the canonical source of truth. |
| **Duplicate check-in rejected (`409 Conflict`)** | Employee already clocked in. | The system detected status `IN`. If the employee is departing, select the **Check-Out** action button. |
| **`{Verify Link}` tag renders plain text or missing** | Malformed tag syntax. | Use standard enclosure formats: `{Verify Link}`, `{{Verify Link}}`, or `${Verify Link}`. |
