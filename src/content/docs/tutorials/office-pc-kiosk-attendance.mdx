---
title: Employee attendance from the office computer
description: Let employees clock in and out from their own office computer with a Google Form and FormMail Hub links. No QR scanner, camera or staff accounts needed.
---

Each employee fills in a Google Form once. They receive an email with two links: one that authorizes their office computer's browser, and one they click every day to check in and out. You and the employee get an email receipt for each action.

You need: a [connected SMTP](/guides/smtp-setup/). Everything else works on the Free plan.

## 1. Create the registration form

1. Create a Google Form with the employee's name and email address.
2. In **Settings**, turn on **Limit to 1 response** so each employee has exactly one attendance record.
3. Connect the form to FormMail Hub. See [Get started](/guides/formmailhub/).

## 2. Create the attendance email

[Create a template](/guides/template-customization/) that contains `{Full Scanner}` and then `{Verify Link}`, with short instructions. For example:

```text
Hi {Full name},

Step 1 (once): on your office computer, open this link to authorize your browser:
{Full Scanner}

Step 2 (every day): open this link to check in when you arrive and check out when you leave.
Bookmark it in the same browser:
{Verify Link}
```

When `{Full Scanner}` is sent to the person who submitted the form, it only allows them to check **their own** record in and out. You do not need to add employees as team members.

## 3. Send it automatically

[Create a rule](/guides/conditional-logic/) of type **Always Notify** that sends this template to the employee's email field.

## 4. What employees do

1. Open the email on their office computer and click the **Step 1** link once, in the browser they normally use. That browser is now authorized for 7 days. Opening the link again renews it.
2. Each day, open the **Step 2** link (or the bookmark) and click **Check In** on arrival, then **Check Out** before leaving.

The system refuses a second check-in before a check-out, and a check-out before a check-in. If someone opens another person's link, they can see the record but cannot change it.

## 5. Receipts and records

After every check-in or check-out:

- The employee receives a confirmation with the time and the location when available.
- An audit email is sent to the employee with a copy to the form admin.

Each record is kept for 30 days after its last change.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| The page says the device is not authorized | Open the Step 1 link again, in the same browser used for Step 2. Links opened inside an email app authorize only that app. |
| "Self Check-in Restricted" | The browser was authorized by another employee's link. Each employee should use their own computer and links. |
| An employee has two records | Turn on **Limit to 1 response** in the form settings. |
| Tags appear as plain text in the email | Check the spelling, for example `{Full Scanner}` and `{Verify Link}`. |
