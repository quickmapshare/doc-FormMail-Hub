This step-by-step tutorial guides Administrators through setting up an **Office PC Kiosk Attendance Station** or enabling **Direct Self-Service Check-In** for employees using **FormMail Hub**.

By combining Google Forms registration, Custom SMTP delivery, stateless QR passes with direct verification links (`{Verify Link}`), and browser-based staff authorization cookies, you can turn any office PC or front-desk workstation into an automated clock-in/out station—without purchasing proprietary hardware or installing separate software.

To test form submission triggers before rolling out your office station, explore our interactive [Live Demo Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSc2lkYREd5ePz521uYfBDeumOOoPKeBP87i1aSpwokHdFMIHw/viewform) or install the add-on directly via the [Google Workspace Marketplace Listing](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/formmail_hub/409227874327).

## 2. Step-by-Step Implementation Guide

### Step 1: Configure Google Form Settings & Initial Registration
1. Open Google Forms™ and create your employee attendance or intake form.
2. Navigate to **Settings > Responses** and toggle ON **"Limit to 1 response"** (this ensures each employee holds a unique, single reference ticket code).
3. Connect the form to FormMail Hub via the Google Forms™ entry add-on or Google Sheets™ add-on.

### Step 2: Configure Custom SMTP & Team Authorization
1. In the FormMail Hub UI, navigate to **Settings > SMTP Config**.
2. Input your Custom SMTP credentials (e.g., **Gmail™ App Password**, **Amazon SES**, **SendGrid**, or corporate relay) and click **Test Connection**.
   > **Important Rule:** Admins **MUST** configure and test custom SMTP settings before unlocking Templates, Rules, Campaigns, or Analytics. Until custom SMTP is active, these feature tabs remain strictly locked in the UI.
3. In the **Team Members** section, add the email address for the manager, supervisor, or office kiosk operator account.
4. FormMail Hub generates signed Authorization Links (`{Check-in Scanner}`, `{Check-out Scanner}`, or `{Full Scanner}`) for authorized staff members.

### Step 3: Design Email Template with QR Ticket & Direct Verification Link
1. Go to **Templates** > **+ Create New Template**.
2. Set up an Auto-responder template and insert the dynamic ticket tags:
   - `{QR Code}`: Renders the visual QR badge for scanning via webcam/scanner device.
   - `{Verify Link}`: Renders a direct clickable verification link (`/qr-verify?t=...`) for instant camera-free self-check-in on PC or mobile browsers.
   - `{Form title}`: Displays the official form title.
   - `{Unsubscribe Link}`: Provides the form-specific opt-out link.

3. Go to **Rules** > Create an Auto-responder Rule triggered on form submission to automatically dispatch this ticket to registering employees using your verified Custom SMTP route.

### Step 4: Authorize & Setup the Office PC Kiosk Browser
1. On the dedicated office desktop PC, open the designated browser (e.g., Google Chrome).
2. Copy and paste the authorized `{Full Scanner}` (or `{Check-in Scanner}`) Auth Link generated for the team member into this browser address bar and press Enter.
   > **Browser Session Isolation Note:** Authorization relies on an HMAC-SHA256 signed cookie set during link activation (`/qr-auth?t=...`). Opening the link in one browser (e.g., in an email client) will **NOT** authorize a different browser. You **MUST** paste the Auth link directly into the exact browser application that will be used for scanning.
3. Bookmark the scanning endpoint page (`/qr-verify`) on the browser's bookmark toolbar or set it as the default homepage.

### Step 5: Daily Attendance Execution Workflow

Employees have two convenient options to complete daily check-in and check-out:

#### Option A: QR Scan via Office PC Camera or Barcode Reader
1. Arriving employee presents their printed or mobile `{QR Code}` ticket to the office PC webcam or USB 2D barcode scanner.
2. The authorized browser processes the scan at `/api/qr/action`.
3. The live status badge updates instantly on screen (`🟢 Status: Checked-In` or `🚪 Status: Checked-Out`).
4. Redis key `checkin:{form_id}:{refCode}` updates state to `IN` (or `OUT` upon departure) and refreshes TTL to **30 days (2,592,000 seconds)**.

#### Option B: Direct Self-Service Verification Link via Email
1. Employee opens their registration confirmation email on their PC or phone.
2. Employee clicks the `{Verify Link}` dynamic link.
3. The system executes the state transition (`IN` / `OUT`) based on current status and context.

### Step 6: Automated Dual Audit Receipts
Upon every successful scan or manual verification:
1. **Employee Attendance Receipt:** Sent immediately to the employee confirming their status change (`Checked In` / `Checked Out`) with form title, ticket reference code, and UTC timestamp.
2. **Admin & Operator Audit Trail:** Dispatched immediately to the scanning operator / Team Member and **CC'd to the Form Admin** for compliance monitoring.

## 3. Atomic State Concurrency & 30-Day TTL Rules

The attendance backend guarantees data integrity through strict state transition checks:

| Action | Current Ticket State | Engine Behavior | Key TTL Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Check-in** | `OUT` or Not Scanned | Updates status to `IN`, logs operator email & timestamp. | Refreshes to 30 Days (2,592,000s) |
| **Check-in** | `IN` | **Rejected with `409 Conflict`** (prevents duplicate clock-ins). | Unchanged (keeps existing state) |
| **Check-out** | `IN` | Validates active status, updates status to `OUT`, logs operator & timestamp. | Refreshes to 30 Days (2,592,000s) |
| **Check-out** | `OUT` or Not Scanned | Denied (ticket must be checked in first). | Unchanged |

## 4. Troubleshooting & Operational FAQ

| Issue / Symptom | Root Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Feature Locked" message in sidebar** | Custom SMTP not yet tested or verified. | Admins MUST configure and test Custom SMTP under **Settings > SMTP Config** to unlock Templates, Rules, Campaigns, and Analytics. |
| **Office PC shows "Unauthorized Scanner"** | Auth cookie missing on this browser instance. | Copy the `{Full Scanner}` link from the team email and paste it directly into the office PC's browser address bar. |
| **Employee registered multiple times** | "Limit to 1 response" disabled in Google Forms. | Turn ON "Limit to 1 response" in Google Forms Settings > Responses. The backend uses `checkin:{form_id}:{refCode}` as the canonical source of truth. |
| **Duplicate check-in rejected (`409 Conflict`)** | Employee already clocked in. | The system detected status `IN`. If the employee is departing, select the **Check-Out** button. |
| **`{Verify Link}` tag renders plain text or missing** | Malformed tag syntax. | Use standard enclosure formats: `{Verify Link}`, `{{Verify Link}}`, or `${Verify Link}`. |
