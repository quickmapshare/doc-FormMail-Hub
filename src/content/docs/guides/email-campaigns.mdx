While real-time transactional auto-responders send single emails immediately upon form completion, **FormMail Hub's Broadcast Campaign Engine** enables you to engage your collected lead database with targeted newsletters, product updates, event announcements, and follow-up email series.

*Note: Bulk email broadcasting (Campaigns) is **ONLY** available when launching FormMail Hub from the **Google Sheets core add-on**. It is not accessible from Google Forms entry add-ons.*

---

## 1. Automated Lead Indexing & Contact Management

Every time a respondent fills out a Google Form™ connected to FormMail Hub, the system automatically indexes their response into a central subscriber list within the backing Google Sheet™.

### Key Capabilities of Contact Indexing
- **Zero Manual Exporting:** Eliminates the need to download CSV files from Google Sheets™ or copy-paste email addresses into external CRM platforms.
- **Metadata Mapping:** Captures submission timestamps, individual field responses (e.g., job title, location, interest tier), and consent status.
- **Deduplication:** Automatically handles duplicate form submissions from the same respondent, maintaining a clean unique contact registry.

## 2. Audience Segmentation & Dynamic Filters

Sending generic mass emails to your entire contact list can lower engagement and increase spam complaints. FormMail Hub allows you to filter and segment collected leads using submission data stored in your Google Sheet.

### Common Segmentation Rules
- **Date-Based Filtering:** Target respondents who submitted forms within a specific date range (e.g., leads collected in Q1 or within the last 30 days).
- **Response Value Matching:** Filter contacts based on specific form choices (e.g., target only users who selected `"Interested in Enterprise Plan"` or `"Location = New York"`).
- **Opt-In Status Filter:** Automatically exclude addresses that have unsubscribed or bounced during previous campaigns.

## 3. Step-by-Step Walkthrough: Creating & Dispatching a Campaign

Follow this workflow to create, synchronize, and dispatch a targeted broadcast campaign to form respondents:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Create Campaign Template  -->  2. Configure Filtering Rules in Google Sheet    |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                          |
                                          v
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3. Enable Rule as Active     -->  4. Open Sheets Add-on & Switch to Campaign View |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                          |
                                          v
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 5. Select Synchronized Name  -->  6. Click 'Dispatch' Button to Initiate Bulk Send|
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Step 1: Create a Campaign Template
1. In the FormMail Hub interface, navigate to the template builder.
2. Draft your email message using Rich Text or HTML formatting.
3. Incorporate dynamic personalization tags (e.g., `{First Name}`, `{Company Name}`) and mandatory compliance tags (`{Unsubscribe link}`).
4. Save the campaign template.

### Step 2: Configure Filtering Rules
1. Define segmentation criteria targeting specific respondent rows in the backing responses Google Sheet™ (e.g., `Plan = Enterprise` AND `Date >= 2026-01-01`).
2. Map the template variables to the corresponding spreadsheet response columns.

### Step 3: Enable the Active Campaign Rule
1. Review rule parameters and toggle the rule state to **Active**.
2. This synchronizes the campaign configuration with the backing response sheet.

### Step 4: Open FormMail Hub in Google Sheets & Switch to Campaign View
1. Open the backing responses **Google Sheet™**.
2. Launch the **FormMail Hub** add-on (Campaign sending is strictly supported in the Google Sheets core add-on).
3. Switch the interface view to the **Campaign** view.

### Step 5: Select Synchronized Campaign & Dispatch
1. Locate and select the synchronized campaign name from the campaign list.
2. Review the final calculated audience summary.
3. Click the **Dispatch** button to initiate the bulk email broadcast.

## 4. Compliance, Opt-Out Handling & Suppression Lists

FormMail Hub handles compliance automatically to protect your domain's reputation and adhere to global email privacy standards.

### How `{Unsubscribe link}` Works
1. When a recipient clicks `{Unsubscribe link}`, they are directed to a hosted secure confirmation page.
2. Upon confirmation, their email address is flagged as `SUPPRESSED` in your FormMail Hub database.
3. **Automated Blocking:** Future broadcast campaigns and transactional auto-responders automatically skip suppressed email addresses, returning an internal log status of `OPT_OUT_SKIPPED`.

### Managing Your Suppression List
- View, search, or manually add unsubscribed contacts under **Campaigns** > **Suppression List** in the Google Sheets core add-on.
- Export suppression records for internal compliance audits.

## 5. High-Volume Dispatch & Rate Limiting

When sending broadcasts to thousands of contacts, provider daily limits must be managed:

| Sending Route | Max Daily Broadcast Quota | Best Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Free Gmail™ SMTP** | 500 emails / day | Suitable for small lists and community notifications. |
| **Google Workspace SMTP** | Up to 1,500 emails / day | Good for medium business outreach. |
| **Amazon SES / SendGrid** | 10,000+ emails / day | Essential for high-volume marketing broadcasts and transactional series. |

*Tip: Connect Amazon SES or SendGrid under **Settings > Custom SMTP** before scheduling dispatches exceeding 500 recipients to avoid standard Google rate limit errors (`ERR_QUOTA_EXCEEDED`).*

## 6. Campaign Analytics & Performance Diagnostics

After clicking **Dispatch** in the Google Sheets core add-on, track real-time delivery status in the **Campaign Analytics Dashboard**:

- **Delivered Rate:** Percentage of messages successfully accepted by recipient mail servers.
- **Hard / Soft Bounces:** Invalid email addresses are categorized and added to the diagnostic log.
- **Unsubscribe Rate:** Track opt-out counts per campaign to measure content relevance.
- **Retry Queue Status:** If a remote SMTP connection experiences temporary throttles, FormMail Hub buffers remaining messages and retries automatically.
