While real-time transactional auto-responders deliver single emails upon form completion, **FormMail Hub's Broadcast Campaign Engine** enables Administrators to send targeted bulk communications to collected respondents.

---

## 1. Prerequisites & Ecosystem Constraints

Before launching a broadcast campaign, the following requirements must be met:

1. **Custom SMTP Requirement:** Admins **MUST** configure custom SMTP settings before creating email templates, rules, campaigns, or accessing analytics, as these features require sending emails under the Admin's own email identity.
2. **Google Sheets Core Add-on Exclusivity:** Bulk sending (Campaigns) is **ONLY** available when launched from the **FormMail Hub Google Sheets add-on**. It is not accessible from Google Forms entry add-ons because broad audience segmentation, cell-vector list queries, and batch execution require Google Sheets environment capabilities.

---

## 2. Campaign Creation & Dispatch Workflow

To send a proactive campaign to form respondents, the Admin follows this exact workflow:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Admin creates campaign template (incorporating required {Unsubscribe link})    |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                          |
                                          v
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2. Admin configures filtering rules to target respondents in responses Sheet     |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                          |
                                          v
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3. Admin enables the rule as an active campaign                                   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                          |
                                          v
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4. Admin opens FormMail Hub Google Sheets add-on on responses sheet & switches   |
|    to Campaign view                                                               |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                          |
                                          v
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 5. Admin selects the synchronized campaign name                                  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                          |
                                          v
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 6. Admin clicks 'Dispatch' button to initiate bulk email dispatch                |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Detailed Workflow Steps:
1. **Create Campaign Template:** The Admin navigates to the template builder, drafts the message content, and inserts mandatory compliance tags (`{Unsubscribe link}`) alongside dynamic tags (`{First Name}`).
2. **Configure Filtering Rules:** In rule settings, set targeting criteria against the backing responses Google Sheet™ (e.g., `Submission Date >= 2026-01-01` or `Product Interest Equals Enterprise`).
3. **Enable Active Campaign Rule:** Toggle the rule state to **Active** to synchronize it with the spreadsheet.
4. **Open Google Sheets Core Add-on & Switch to Campaign View:** Open the responses Google Sheet™, launch the **FormMail Hub** Google Sheets add-on, and switch the interface view to **Campaign**.
5. **Select Synchronized Campaign Name:** Choose the synchronized campaign name from the dropdown selection list.
6. **Click 'Dispatch':** Click the **Dispatch** button to initiate the bulk email dispatch process.

---

## 3. Compliance & Suppression Registry

- **Mandatory Unsubscribe Link:** Broadcast campaign templates require the `{Unsubscribe link}` tag.
- **Opt-Out Handling:** When a recipient clicks the unsubscribe link, their email address is flagged as `SUPPRESSED`.
- **Automatic Suppression Enforcement:** During campaign dispatch, the system queries the suppression registry and skips unsubscribed addresses, logging an `OPT_OUT_SKIPPED` event status.

---

## 4. Monitoring Dispatch Performance

Once **Dispatch** is clicked, the Admin can monitor batch processing and analytics within the Google Sheets core add-on:

- **Delivered Count:** Successfully accepted messages by downstream mail servers.
- **Bounces & Errors:** Tracked via hard (`BOUNCED_HARD`) and soft (`BOUNCED_SOFT`) status codes.
- **Opt-Out Counts:** Total unsubscribes recorded for the campaign run.
