---
title: Send email campaigns from Google Sheets
description: Send a bulk email to selected Google Form respondents from the FormMail Hub Google Sheets add-on, using a template and filter conditions.
---

A campaign sends one template to the respondents in your responses spreadsheet who match your conditions. You start it yourself, from the **FormMail Hub add-on in Google Sheets™**. Campaigns cannot be started from the Google Forms add-on.

:::note[Before you start]
- Link your form to a Google Sheet (in Google Forms: **Responses → Link to Sheets**).
- [Connect your SMTP](/guides/smtp-setup/) and [create the template](/guides/template-customization/) you want to send.
:::

## Set up the campaign

1. In **Manage Rules**, create a rule with the campaign template, the respondent's email field as recipient, and conditions that select who should receive it. See [Rules and conditions](/guides/conditional-logic/).
2. Open **Campaigns**, select the rule and turn on **Enable as Campaign**. You can also set an **End Date** for the campaign.

## Send it

1. Open the responses spreadsheet, make the responses tab of your form the active tab, and start the FormMail Hub add-on (**Extensions → FormMail Hub**).
2. In the sidebar, click **Campaigns**.
3. Choose your campaign in the list and click **Dispatch Campaign**.

The add-on sends up to 100 rows at a time. It uses the **FormMail Hub** column to decide which rows to send:

- Rows where that column is empty, or shows an error, are sent.
- Rows where that column already has a value are skipped. This includes rows that the add-on marked `done` when the response arrived, and rows already sent by an earlier dispatch, which show the campaign's name.
- After a row is sent, the add-on writes the campaign's name in that column. Clicking Dispatch again therefore does not send the row a second time.
- If a batch fails, the rows already sent keep their value, and the rest stay as they were.

So a campaign reaches only the rows that were still empty or in error when you dispatch it.

FormMail Hub sends the emails in the background through your SMTP. Respondents who have unsubscribed from this form are skipped.

## Good practice

- Include `{Unsubscribe link}` in campaign templates.
- Test the conditions on a copy of the sheet, or with a condition that matches only your own response, before sending to everyone.
- Large campaigns can reach your provider's daily limit. Use a sending service for large lists. See [sending limits](/guides/smtp-setup/#sending-limits).

The Free plan sends up to 300 campaign emails per day. See [Plans & pricing](/guides/plans-and-pricing/).
