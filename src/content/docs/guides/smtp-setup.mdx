---
title: Connect your SMTP
description: Connect Gmail, Google Workspace, Amazon SES, SendGrid, Mailgun or your own mail server so FormMail Hub can send your templates, rules and campaigns.
---

FormMail Hub sends your own emails (templates, auto-replies, rules and campaigns) through an email account you connect with SMTP. Emails then come from your address, and your provider's sending limits apply. Connecting SMTP is **free on every plan**.

Until SMTP is connected and tested, the **Templates**, **Rules**, **Campaigns** and **Analytics** pages stay unavailable. Submission alerts to you and your team do not need SMTP.

## Connect and test

1. In the dashboard, open **SMTP Config**.
2. Fill in **SMTP Server (Host)**, **Port**, **Username** and **SMTP Password / Token** using the table below, then click **Save Settings**.
3. Under **Connection Test**, enter the sender address and an address to receive the test, then click **Send Test**.

When the test email arrives, the status changes to **Verified & Ready** and the other pages unlock.

## Settings for common providers

| Provider | Host | Port | Username | Password |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gmail / Google Workspace | `smtp.gmail.com` | 587 or 465 | your full email address | a 16-character **App Password** |
| Amazon SES | `email-smtp.<region>.amazonaws.com` | 587 | SES SMTP username | SES SMTP password |
| SendGrid | `smtp.sendgrid.net` | 587 | `apikey` | your API key |
| Mailgun | `smtp.mailgun.org` | 587 | your SMTP login | your SMTP password |
| Your own server | from your host | 587 or 465 | from your host | from your host |

### Gmail App Password

Gmail does not accept your normal password over SMTP. You need an App Password:

1. Turn on 2-Step Verification in your [Google Account security settings](https://myaccount.google.com/security).
2. Search for **App passwords**, create one named "FormMail Hub", and copy the 16-character code.
3. Paste it into **SMTP Password / Token** without spaces.

If App passwords is missing on a Google Workspace account, your administrator may have turned it off.

### Providers that use an API key

With SendGrid, Amazon SES and similar services, the sender address must be one you have verified with that provider. Enter it in the **Sender Email** field of each rule.

## Sending limits

Your provider decides how many emails you can send per day. Gmail and Google Workspace accounts have daily limits that are fine for most forms. For large events or campaigns, use a sending service such as Amazon SES, SendGrid or Mailgun. Check your provider's current limits on its website.

FormMail Hub's own plan limits are on [Plans & pricing](/guides/plans-and-pricing/).

## If the test fails

| What you see | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authentication error (often code 535) | Wrong username or password, or a normal Gmail password | Check the username; for Gmail, create a new App Password |
| Connection timed out | Wrong host or port | Use port 587, or 465 if your provider requires SSL |
| Test sent but never arrives | Spam folder, or unverified sender address | Check spam; verify the sender address with your provider |
