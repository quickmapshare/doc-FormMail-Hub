title: Analytics and daily reports
description: See how many emails each form sent, opened and failed in FormMail Hub, and receive a daily summary report by email.
---

FormMail Hub counts every email it sends for each form, every day, and sends the form admin a summary each morning.

## The Analytics page

Open **Analytics** and choose a form. Like Templates and Rules, this page unlocks after [SMTP is connected](/guides/smtp-setup/).

You see today's usage against your [plan's daily limits](/guides/plans-and-pricing/), and totals for:

- **Form submissions** received.
- **Submission alerts**: sent, failed, and success rate.
- **Rule emails**: triggered, sent, opened and failed.
- **Campaign emails**: campaigns run and emails sent.

Figures are split into three kinds of email:

| Kind | What it includes |
| --- | --- |
| System | Submission alerts, daily reports and error alerts sent by FormMail Hub |
| Automation | Emails sent by your rules when someone submits the form |
| Campaign | Bulk emails you dispatch from Google Sheets |

Opens are counted when the recipient's email app loads images. Some apps block images, so the real number of opens can be higher.

## Daily summary report

Shortly after midnight in each form's time zone, the form admin receives an email summarizing the previous day's numbers for that form.

Each report has an unsubscribe link that stops reports **for that form only**. Reports for your other forms continue.

## Other email preferences

- **Respondents** can stop emails from a form through `{Unsubscribe link}` in your templates. It applies to that form only.
- **SMTP error alerts** are sent to the admin when your SMTP connection fails. Their unsubscribe link stops these alerts for all your forms.
