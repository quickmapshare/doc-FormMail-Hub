A template is the subject and body of an email you want to send. Templates are used by [rules](/guides/conditional-logic/) (sent when someone submits the form) and by [campaigns](/guides/email-campaigns/).

:::note[Before you start]
[Connect your SMTP](/guides/smtp-setup/) first. The Email Templates page unlocks after the SMTP test succeeds.
:::

## Create a template

1. Open **Email Templates** and click **Create** (new template).
2. Enter a **Template Name** (only you see it), the email **Subject** and the **Body**.
3. Pick a design theme, or keep **Plain Text**. Ten themes are available, from business to newsletter styles.
4. Save. The template is ready to use in a rule.

The Free plan allows 5 templates per form. Pro removes this limit. See [Plans & pricing](/guides/plans-and-pricing/).

A template that is used by an active rule cannot be deleted. Remove it from the rule first.

## Dynamic tags

Tags are replaced with real values when the email is sent. You can write a tag as `{Tag}`, `{{Tag}}` or `${Tag}`.

### Form answers

Write the exact title of any question in braces. For a question titled *First name*, `{First name}` inserts the respondent's answer.

### Built-in tags

| Tag | Inserts |
| --- | --- |
| `{Form title}` | The title of the form |
| `{Linked form}` | A link to the form |
| `{All fields}` | A table of every question and answer |
| `{Unsubscribe link}` | A link that stops future emails from this form to the recipient |
| `{QR Code}` | A QR ticket image, used for [check-in](/tutorials/qr-event-ticketing/) |
| `{Verify Link}` | The same ticket as a clickable link, for check-in without a camera |
| `{Check-in Scanner}` | A link that lets the recipient's browser check tickets in |
| `{Check-out Scanner}` | A link that lets the recipient's browser check tickets out |
| `{Full Scanner}` | A link that allows both check-in and check-out |

The three scanner tags behave differently depending on who receives the email:

- **Team members** get a staff link that lets them scan anyone's ticket for that form.
- **The respondent** gets a link that lets them check only their own ticket in and out. This is how [office attendance](/tutorials/office-pc-kiosk-attendance/) works.

Opening a scanner link authorizes that browser for 7 days. After that, open the link again.

## Tips

- Copy question titles exactly, including capital letters and punctuation. A tag that does not match any question is not replaced.
- Add `{Unsubscribe link}` to emails you send to respondents, especially campaigns.
- Send yourself a test submission after creating a rule, to check the result before sharing the form.
