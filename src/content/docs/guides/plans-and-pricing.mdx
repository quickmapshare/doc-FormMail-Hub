## 2. Choosing the Right Plan for Your Workflow

### When Free is the Perfect Fit
- **Classrooms & Community Clubs:** Perfect for sending standard registration confirmations or simple acknowledgment receipts to students or participants.
- **Single Department Intakes:** Ideal for basic contact forms or suggestion boxes that require instant administrative notifications and a standard respondent auto-reply.
- **Getting Started:** Test custom SMTP delivery, design email templates, and evaluate daily summary reports without any upfront cost.

### When to Upgrade to Pro
- **Event Ticketing & Access Control:** Organizations issuing digital `{QR Code}` tickets, generating zero-login `{Full Scanner}` authorization passes, and verifying attendee check-in/check-out in real-time.
- **Workplace & School Attendance:** HR teams and educators running camera-free office desktop kiosk clock-in systems or student lecture tracking.
- **Multi-Department Conditional Routing:** Businesses that need multi-condition rules (e.g., routing billing inquiries to Finance and technical tickets to Engineering).
- **Branded & High-Volume Email Campaigns:** Marketing teams and businesses sending unbranded, professional broadcast campaigns and auto-responders to thousands of recipients without daily platform caps.
- **Multi-Staff Event Operations:** Teams needing to assign staff scanners without exposing administrative access or system settings.

## 4. How to Upgrade to Pro (Step-by-Step)

Upgrading your account takes less than two minutes:

1. Open your connected Google Form™ or Google Sheet™.
2. Launch the **FormMail Hub** add-on sidebar.
3. Click **"Go Beyond"** (Google Forms™) or **"Open Dashboard"** (Google Sheets™) to launch your cloud management interface.
4. In the top navigation or account banner, click the **"Upgrade to Pro"** button.
5. Select your preferred billing frequency:
   - **Monthly Billing:** $5 / month
   - **Yearly Billing:** $39 / year *(Best Value)*
6. Complete the secure checkout via PayPal.
7. Upon successful payment verification, your account is instantly upgraded to Pro—unlocking unlimited rules, unlimited templates, QR attendance, conditional logic, team member management, and unbranded emails immediately.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (Billing & Plans)

### Q: Does the Free plan require a credit card to get started?
**A:** No. You can install FormMail Hub and start sending automated emails on the Free plan without providing any payment information.

### Q: Are sending quotas on the Pro plan truly unlimited?
**A:** Yes. FormMail Hub places no daily platform sending limits on Pro users. Because auto-responders and bulk campaigns are sent via your configured Custom SMTP (e.g., Amazon SES, SendGrid, Mailgun, or Google Workspace), your sending capacity is governed entirely by your SMTP provider's own sending limits.

### Q: What happens to my templates and rules if I cancel my Pro subscription?
**A:** If your subscription expires, your account returns to the Free plan. Existing templates and rules remain safely saved in your account. However, advanced Pro features (such as conditional logic evaluation, QR ticket generation, and team scanner authorization) will pause until Pro is reactivated.

### Q: Can multiple editors on the same Google Form share a single Pro account?
**A:** In FormMail Hub, only the **Primary Connector** (the first user among the form's editors who connects that form) configures rules, templates, and SMTP settings. If the Primary Connector holds a Pro subscription, that form enjoys full Pro capabilities, including team member scanner assignments.
