title: Plans and pricing
description: Compare the FormMail Hub Free and Pro plans, including daily email limits, template and rule limits, and how to upgrade or cancel.
---

Every feature, including SMTP, conditional rules, QR tickets, team members and campaigns, is available on the **Free** plan. **Pro** removes the limits and the FormMail Hub footer.

| | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $0 | **$5 / month** or **$39 / year** |
| Templates | 5 per form | No limit |
| Rules | 6 per form | No limit |
| Submission alerts | 100 per day | No FormMail Hub limit |
| Rule emails (auto-replies) | 200 per day | No FormMail Hub limit |
| Campaign emails | 300 per day | No FormMail Hub limit |
| FormMail Hub footer in emails | Yes | No |
| Custom SMTP, conditions, delays | ✅ | ✅ |
| QR tickets and check-in | ✅ | ✅ |
| Team members | ✅ | ✅ |
| Analytics and daily reports | ✅ | ✅ |

Daily email limits count all forms in your account and reset every day. On Pro, your SMTP provider's own sending limits still apply. See [sending limits](/guides/smtp-setup/#sending-limits).

When you reach a limit on the Free plan, FormMail Hub warns you by email. Emails over the daily limit are skipped, not postponed, so upgrade before a busy day such as an event.

## Upgrade

1. [Connect your SMTP](/guides/smtp-setup/) if you have not already. The upgrade button appears after the SMTP test succeeds.
2. Click **Upgrade** in the dashboard header, or open **Plan & Billing** in the sidebar.
3. Choose monthly or yearly and pay with PayPal.

Pro applies to your whole account, so every form you manage gets the Pro limits.

## Cancel

Cancel at any time from **Plan & Billing**. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for, then the account returns to the Free plan. Payments are not refunded, so try the Free plan first.

Questions about billing? [Contact support](https://formmail.vietutd.com/contact).
