Managing form submission alerts in organizational settings requires delivering notifications to designated team members without exposing system configurations or consuming personal email daily limits.

---

## 1. Role & Permissions Architecture

FormMail Hub enforces strict role boundaries across all form workflows:

### Primary Connector / Admin Role
Only the **first user among the form's editors who connects that form to FormMail Hub** is granted exclusive rights to configure settings, templates, rules, custom SMTP, and triggers for that form. Co-editors on the form or sheet do not automatically receive administrative rights.

### Team Member Logic
Team members are **ONLY** added so the Admin can select who receives email notifications upon form submission. Team members **DO NOT** have edit or configuration permissions.

| Capability / Action | Primary Connector (Admin) | Team Members |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Granted Exclusive Rights (First Form Editor to Connect)** | ✅ | ❌ |
| **Configure System & Custom SMTP Settings** | ✅ | ❌ |
| **Create & Edit Email Templates & Rules** | ✅ | ❌ |
| **Add / Remove Team Member Email Addresses** | ✅ | ❌ |
| **Receive Selected Email Submission Alerts** | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Launch Bulk Campaigns (Sheets Add-on)** | ✅ | ❌ |

## 2. Notification Delivery Mechanics

FormMail Hub handles internal team notifications separately from customer-facing custom email triggers:

### Built-in System SMTP Scope (Out-of-the-Box)
Built-in System SMTP is **STRICTLY** restricted to sending default-templated submission alerts to the Form Admin and selected Team Members (serving as a live demo and conserving Admin SMTP quota). System SMTP **CANNOT** be used for custom templates, rule-based notifications, or respondent auto-responders.

### Custom Team Notification Routing
If the Admin wishes to send custom templates or rule-based notifications to team members:
- Admins **MUST** configure and test custom SMTP settings before unlocking Templates, Rules, Campaigns, or Analytics. Until custom SMTP is active, these feature tabs remain strictly locked in the UI.
- Once unlocked, the Admin can set up multi-condition rules routing custom templates to specific team members based on submission values using custom SMTP.

## 3. Adding Team Members (Admin Workflow)

Only the Primary Connector (Admin) can manage the team member roster:

1. Launch **FormMail Hub** from Google Forms™ or Google Sheets™.
2. Navigate to **Team Members** in the sidebar.
3. Enter the email address of the team member (e.g., `colleague@yourcompany.com`).
4. Click **Add Team Member**.

Once added, the team member's address becomes selectable by the Admin for receiving notification alerts upon form submission.
