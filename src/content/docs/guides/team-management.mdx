In enterprise and organizational environments, managing form submission alerts often requires delivering notifications to specific team members. **FormMail Hub** enables account administrators to manage a team list strictly for routing submission email notifications without sharing account credentials or consuming personal daily email quotas.

---

## 1. Team Collaboration & Permission Model

FormMail Hub maintains strict role boundaries between the account administrator and team members:

- **Admin Role:** Only the primary account/workspace Admin can configure settings, email templates, conditional rules, triggers, and broadcast campaigns.
- **Team Members:** Team members are added **ONLY** so the Admin can select who receives specific email notifications upon form submission. Team members do **NOT** have permission to edit settings, configure templates/triggers, or modify forms and campaigns.

### Key Advantages of Team Member Notifications
- **Zero Quota Consumption for Internal Alerts:** Internal notification alerts sent to team members are routed through FormMail Hub's dedicated internal delivery infrastructure. This preserves 100% of your personal or custom SMTP sending quota for customer-facing auto-responders and marketing campaigns.
- **Targeted Email Routing:** The Admin can attach specific team members to conditional logic rules or global submission alerts based on form response values.

## 2. Adding Team Members (Admin Only)

Only the primary account/workspace Admin can add or remove team member email addresses from the team roster.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Launch **FormMail Hub** from your Google Form™ or connected Google Sheet™.
2. Open the main dashboard sidebar or click **Dashboard**.
3. In the left navigation menu, navigate to **Team Members**.
4. In the **Add Team Member** field, enter the team member's email address (e.g., `colleague@yourcompany.com`).
5. Click **Add Team Member**.

Once added, the team member's address becomes available in dropdown menus across notification settings and conditional logic rules so the Admin can route email alerts to them.

## 3. Routing Submission Alerts to Team Members

After adding team members, the Admin can configure real-time notification rules to dispatch submission alerts to selected team recipients:

### Scenario A: Global Internal Alerts (All Submissions)
To send notification emails to designated team members whenever a form is submitted:
1. Navigate to **Email Rules > Internal Alerts**.
2. Under **Recipients**, select the team member email addresses who should receive the submission details.
3. Select or assign the desired internal notification template (e.g., containing the `{Form Summary}` tag).
4. Save the rule. Notifications will be dispatched via FormMail Hub's dedicated system relay, consuming **0%** of your personal or SMTP daily quota.

### Scenario B: Conditional Team Routing
To route submission alerts to specific team members based on user responses (e.g., assigning support tiers or department heads):
1. Navigate to **Email Rules > Conditional Rules**.
2. Create a conditional rule (e.g., `If "Department" Equals "Sales"`).
3. Set the **To Recipient** to the specific sales team member's email address.
4. Save and activate the rule.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

### FAQ

**Q: Can team members log in and edit templates or settings?**  
A: No. Team members do not have permission to edit settings, configure templates/triggers, or modify forms/campaigns. They are added solely to serve as designated recipients for specific email notifications configured by the Admin.

**Q: Who can manage settings, templates, and triggers?**  
A: Only the primary account/workspace Admin can configure settings, templates, triggers, and campaigns.

**Q: Why are team members not receiving email alerts?**  
A: Ensure the Admin has selected the team member's address in the active Email Rule or Conditional Rule, and verify that the address is spelled correctly. Also check that internal mail filters allow system relay notifications.
