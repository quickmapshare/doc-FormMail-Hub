In enterprise and organizational environments, managing form submission alerts often requires delivering targeted email notifications to specific team members. **FormMail Hub** enables the form Administrator to manage a team list strictly for routing submission email notifications without sharing account credentials or consuming personal daily email quotas.

---

## 1. Role & Permissions Architecture

FormMail Hub enforces strict, role-based boundaries between the primary Administrator and designated Team Members:

### Primary Connector / Admin Role
Only the **first user among the form's editors who connects that form to FormMail Hub** is granted exclusive rights to configure settings, templates, triggers, and campaigns for that form. Other co-editors on the Google Form or Google Sheet do not automatically inherit administrative rights.

### Team Member Role & Logic
Team members are **ONLY** added so the Admin can select who receives specific email notifications upon form submission. Team members **DO NOT** have permission to edit, configure settings, or modify forms and campaigns.

### Key Advantages of Team Member Notification Routing
- **Zero Quota Consumption for Internal Alerts:** Internal notification alerts dispatched to team members are routed through FormMail Hub's dedicated internal delivery infrastructure. This preserves 100% of your personal or custom SMTP sending quota for customer-facing auto-responders and marketing campaigns.
- **Targeted Departmental Routing:** The Admin can attach specific team members to conditional logic rules or global submission alerts based on response values.

## 2. Adding Team Members (Admin Only)

Only the Primary Connector (Admin) can add or remove team member email addresses from the team roster.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Launch **FormMail Hub** from your Google Form™ or connected Google Sheet™.
2. Open the main dashboard sidebar or click **Dashboard**.
3. In the navigation menu, navigate to **Team Members**.
4. In the **Add Team Member** field, enter the team member's email address (e.g., `colleague@yourcompany.com`).
5. Click **Add Team Member**.

Once added, the team member's email address becomes selectable in dropdown menus across notification settings and conditional logic rules so the Admin can route specific email alerts to them.

## 3. Routing Submission Alerts to Team Members

After adding team members, the Admin can configure real-time notification rules to dispatch submission alerts to selected team recipients:

### Scenario A: Global Internal Alerts (All Submissions)
To send notification emails to designated team members whenever a form is submitted:
1. Navigate to **Email Rules > Internal Alerts**.
2. Under **Recipients**, select the team member email addresses who should receive submission details.
3. Select or assign the desired internal notification template (e.g., containing the `{Form Summary}` tag).
4. Save the rule. Notifications will be dispatched via FormMail Hub's dedicated system relay, consuming **0%** of your personal or SMTP daily quota.

### Scenario B: Conditional Team Routing
To route submission alerts to specific team members based on user responses (e.g., assigning support tiers or department heads):
1. Navigate to **Email Rules > Conditional Rules**.
2. Create a conditional rule (e.g., `If "Department" Equals "Sales"`).
3. Set the **To Recipient** to the specific sales team member's email address.
4. Save and activate the rule.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

### FAQ

**Q: Who gets administrative control over a form's FormMail Hub configuration?**  
A: Only the Primary Connector—the first user among the form's editors who connects that form to FormMail Hub—is granted exclusive rights to configure settings, templates, and triggers for that form.

**Q: Can team members log in and edit templates or settings?**  
A: No. Team members do not have permission to edit, configure settings, or modify forms/campaigns. They are added solely so the Admin can select who receives specific email notifications.

**Q: Why are team members not receiving email alerts?**  
A: Ensure the Admin has added the team member's address under **Team Members** and explicitly selected their address in an active Email Rule or Conditional Rule. Also verify that the address is spelled correctly and that internal corporate spam filters permit system relay dispatches.
