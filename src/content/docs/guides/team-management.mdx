In enterprise environments, managing form submissions and customer outreach is rarely a solo task. **FormMail Hub** includes built-in Team Member Management capabilities, allowing form administrators to invite collaborators, assign granular role permissions, and route specific submission alerts to team members without sharing account credentials or consuming personal daily email quotas.

This guide provides a comprehensive walkthrough of adding team members, configuring multi-user notification workflows, and understanding role access levels across the FormMail Hub ecosystem.

---

## 1. Team Collaboration Architecture

FormMail Hub separates administrative management from internal team notification delivery to ensure efficient team collaboration across your Google Workspace organization.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                              FORMMAIL HUB DASHBOARD                               |
|                  Team Management Console (Dashboard > Team Members)               |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                          |
          +-------------------------------+-------------------------------+
          |                                                               |
          v                                                               v
+-----------------------------------+                           +-----------------------------------+
|       INTERNAL TEAM ALERTS        |                           |     ADMIN & TEAM DASHBOARD        |
|  (Team Member Email Dispatch)     |                           |       (Role-Based Access)         |
+-----------------------------------+                           +-----------------------------------+
| Route: Dedicated System Relay     |                           | Roles: Admin, Member, Viewer      |
| Quota: 0% Personal Quota Consumed |                           | Context: Sheets Core & Forms      |
| Target: Team Email Addresses      |                           | Scope: Form Rules & Response Logs |
+-----------------------------------+                           +-----------------------------------+
```

### Key Architectural Advantages
- **Zero Quota Consumption for Internal Alerts:** Internal alerts sent to team members (`yourname@companyname.com`) are routed through FormMail Hub's dedicated internal delivery infrastructure. This preserves 100% of your personal or custom SMTP sending quota for customer-facing auto-responders and marketing campaigns.
- **Unified Team Index:** Team members added to a workspace can be mapped directly to conditional logic rules, recipient lists, or webhook notification channels.
- **Google Workspace Ecosystem Integration:** Access permissions align seamlessly with Google Workspace domain policies and Google Forms/Sheets authorization levels.

## 2. Ecosystem Context & Feature Availability

Team management capabilities and notification routing operate across both entry-point Google Forms™ add-ons and the core Google Sheets™ application. However, administrative management scope varies depending on where the dashboard is launched:

| Capability / Feature | Google Forms™ Entry Add-ons<br/>*(Form Confirmation, Form to Email, SMTP)* | Core Google Sheets™ Add-on<br/>*(FormMail Hub Core)* |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Invite Team Recipient Addresses** | ✅ Supported | ✅ Supported |
| **Internal Team Submission Alerts** | ✅ Supported (0% Quota Relay) | ✅ Supported (0% Quota Relay) |
| **Assign Member Roles & Permissions** | ✅ Supported | ✅ Supported |
| **Broadcast Campaign Access to Team** | 🚫 **Not Available** | ✅ **Fully Enabled** |
| **View Full Audit Logs & Suppression Index** | ⚠️ Recent Activity Only | ✅ Full Spreadsheet Logs |

*Note: As detailed in the [Ecosystem Architecture](/reference/ecosystem-architecture/), the **Broadcast Campaign Engine (Bulk Sending)** requires direct access to tabular response rows stored in Google Sheets. Therefore, team members can only design or dispatch bulk email campaigns when accessing the dashboard via the **FormMail Hub Google Sheets Core Add-on**.*

## 3. Step-by-Step Walkthrough: Adding & Managing Team Members

Follow these steps to invite new team members and assign appropriate permissions:

### Step 1: Navigate to the Team Members Management Screen
1. Open the **FormMail Hub** add-on from your Google Form™ or connected Google Sheet™.
2. Open the main dashboard sidebar or click **Dashboard**.
3. In the left navigation menu, click **Team Members** (or navigate to `dashboard/team-members`).

### Step 2: Invite a New Team Member
1. On the **Team Members** screen, locate the **Add Team Member** form.
2. In the **Email Address** field, enter your collaborator's corporate email address:
   ```text
   yourname@companyname.com
   ```
3. Select the appropriate **Role** from the dropdown menu:
   - **Admin:** Full read/write access to form triggers, custom SMTP settings, team management, and templates.
   - **Member:** Can edit templates, create conditional logic rules, and view response logs.
   - **Viewer / Recipient:** Restricted to receiving submission notification emails and viewing submission analytics.
4. Click **Add Member** or **Send Invitation**.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| ADD NEW TEAM MEMBER                                                               |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Email Input: [ yourname@companyname.com                                       ]   |
| Role Selection: (o) Admin   ( ) Member   ( ) Viewer / Recipient                   |
|                                                                                   |
| [ + Add Team Member ]                                                             |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Step 3: Verify Invitation Status
Once submitted, the new member will appear in the **Team Members List** with an invitation status indicator:
- **Active:** Member has accepted the invitation or is recognized within the Google Workspace domain.
- **Pending:** Invitation email dispatched; awaiting first login or confirmation.
- **Revoked / Disabled:** Access temporarily suspended by the workspace administrator.

## 5. Routing Submission Alerts to Team Members

Once team members are registered in your workspace, you can easily attach them to real-time submission notification workflows:

### Scenario A: Global Admin Alerts (All Submissions)
To send a summary email to all team admins every time a form is submitted:
1. Navigate to **Email Rules > Admin Alerts**.
2. Under **Recipients**, select **All Active Team Members** or select specific team emails (`yourname@companyname.com`).
3. Select the internal notification template (e.g., *Default Team Alert with `{Form Summary}`*).
4. Save the rule. Notifications will be dispatched via FormMail Hub's dedicated system relay, consuming **0%** of your personal daily email limit.

### Scenario B: Conditional Team Assignment
To route submissions to specific team members based on form choices (e.g., assigning regional leads or specialized technical teams):
1. Navigate to **Email Rules > Conditional Rules**.
2. Create a rule (e.g., `If "Region" Equals "Europe"`).
3. Set the **To Recipient** to the specific European team member's email (`eu-lead@companyname.com`).
4. Save and activate the rule.

## 6. Enterprise Best Practices & Troubleshooting

### Enterprise Security & Domain Alignment
- **Domain Authorization:** When deploying FormMail Hub across large enterprise organizations, Google Workspace Super Admins can perform a **Domain Install** via the Google Workspace Marketplace. This pre-authorizes the application for all team members under `@yourcompany.com`, eliminating individual authorization prompts.
- **Shared Mailboxes & Distribution Lists:** You can add Google Groups or distribution list addresses (e.g., `support-team@companyname.com`) as team recipient entries to distribute submission alerts to entire departments simultaneously.

### Troubleshooting Common Team Management Issues

| Issue / Symptom | Primary Cause | Recommended Remediation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Team member not receiving submission alerts | Email address misspelled or filtered to spam. | Verify address spelling (`yourname@companyname.com`). Ensure corporate mail gateway permits internal relay notifications. |
| Member cannot access Bulk Campaign Engine | Accessing dashboard via Google Forms entry add-on. | Instruct team member to open **FormMail Hub from Google Sheets™** connected to the form responses. |
| "Permission Denied" when editing SMTP settings | User assigned "Member" or "Viewer" role. | Upgrade user role to **Admin** under **Dashboard > Team Members**. |
| Invitation status remains "Pending" | Recipient has not logged into FormMail Hub sidebar. | Member must open FormMail Hub in Google Forms or Sheets at least once to bind OAuth session. |
