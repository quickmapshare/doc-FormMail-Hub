title: Team members and permissions
description: Add team members in FormMail Hub to receive submission alerts, get copies of emails and scan QR tickets. Learn what the form admin and team members can do.
---

Team members are people who receive emails about a form. They do not get access to its settings.

## Who can do what

The first editor of a form who connects it to FormMail Hub becomes its **admin** (shown as *Owner*). Other editors of the Google Form do not get access to its FormMail Hub settings.

| Action | Admin | Team member |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Change SMTP, templates, rules and campaigns | ✅ | ❌ |
| Add or remove team members | ✅ | ❌ |
| Receive submission alerts and email copies | ✅ | ✅ |
| Scan QR tickets with a scanner link | ✅ | ✅ |
| Send campaigns from Google Sheets | ✅ | ❌ |

## Add a team member

1. Open **Team Members** and click **Add Member**.
2. Enter the email address and click **Send Invitation**.
3. The person receives an email and clicks the link to confirm the address.

The status changes from **Pending** to **Verified**. Only verified members can receive alerts or appear in a rule's recipients. If the invitation got lost, click **Resend Email**.

## What team members receive

- **Submission alerts**: on the Submission Notifications page, tick the members who should be alerted about new submissions.
- **Copies of rule emails**: add them under *Internal Recipients (CC)* in a [rule](/guides/conditional-logic/).
- **Scanner links**: put `{Check-in Scanner}`, `{Check-out Scanner}` or `{Full Scanner}` in an email sent to them, and they can [scan tickets](/tutorials/qr-event-ticketing/) from their phone without an account.
