---
title: Rules, conditions and delayed sending
description: Use FormMail Hub rules to send a template to the respondent or your team, only when answers match your conditions, immediately or after a delay.
---

A rule decides **which template** is sent, **to whom**, **under which conditions** and **when**. Each rule sends one template.

:::note[Before you start]
[Connect your SMTP](/guides/smtp-setup/) and [create a template](/guides/template-customization/). The Manage Rules page unlocks after the SMTP test succeeds.
:::

## Create a rule

1. Open **Manage Rules** and create a new rule.
2. Enter a **Rule Name** and choose the template under **Send Email Template**.
3. Set the recipients, conditions and timing described below.
4. Click **Save Rule Configuration**.

The Free plan allows 6 rules per form. Pro removes this limit.

## Rule types

| Type | Sent when |
| --- | --- |
| **Always Notify** | Every submission, without conditions |
| **Automation Rule** | A submission matches the conditions |
| **Manual Campaign** | You click Dispatch in the Google Sheets add-on. See [Email campaigns](/guides/email-campaigns/) |

## Recipients

- **Customer Email Field**: the question that holds the respondent's email address. Choose *Do not send to form submitter* for an internal-only email.
- **Internal Recipients (CC)**: team members who receive a copy. Only [verified team members](/guides/team-management/) appear here.

A rule needs at least one recipient.

Under **Sender Info & Reply-To** you can set the sender name, the sender address and a reply-to address. If your SMTP provider uses an API key (for example SendGrid or Amazon SES), the sender address must be verified with that provider.

## Conditions

Add one or more conditions on the form's answers, then choose how they combine:

- **All conditions match (AND)**: every condition must be true.
- **Any condition matches (OR)**: one true condition is enough.

Available comparisons:

| Comparison | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Equals / Not equals | *Ticket type* equals `VIP` |
| Contains / Does not contain | *Interests* contains `Workshop` |
| Greater than, greater than or equal, less than, less than or equal | *Number of guests* greater than `2` |
| Is empty / Is not empty | *Company* is not empty |

To send different emails depending on the answers, create one rule per case. For example, one rule for `VIP` with the VIP template and another for `Standard`.

## Delayed and scheduled sending

Under **Delay Delivery** you can:

- Wait a number of **seconds, minutes, hours or days** after the submission, for example a reminder one day later.
- Send at a **Fixed Date & Time (Scheduled)**, for example the morning of your event. The time uses the form's time zone.

If someone submits after the scheduled time has passed, that email is skipped rather than sent late.
