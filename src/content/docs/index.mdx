## Custom SMTP Setup & Quota Optimization

Standard Google Forms™ email triggers rely on your default Google Workspace daily sending quotas, which can severely limit outreach for high-volume forms. FormMail Hub resolves this constraint by enabling custom **SMTP integration**, providing complete control over email delivery, sender identity, and daily volume capacity.

### High-Volume Email Delivery Solutions

Connecting your own SMTP server allows you to send thousands of automated auto-responders daily without encountering standard Google Workspace limits:

- **Free Gmail™ SMTP Integration:** Increase standard sending capacity up to **500 custom emails per day** using your own Gmail™ credentials.
- **Transactional Email Providers (Amazon SES, SendGrid, Mailgun):** Scale seamless transactional email delivery up to **10,000+ emails per day** with enterprise-grade deliverability and domain verification.
- **Custom Domain Branding:** Send emails directly from your corporate email domain (e.g., `support@yourcompany.com`) rather than default Google Apps scripts addresses.

### Intelligent Dual-Routing Architecture

To maximize your sending quota efficiency, FormMail Hub separates internal operations from external client communication:

1. **Internal Notification Routing:** Form Mail Hub handles team and administrator submission alerts through a dedicated delivery infrastructure. This ensures zero consumption of your personal or business SMTP daily quota.
2. **Client Auto-Responder Routing:** Customer-facing confirmation emails and auto-responder sequences run strictly through your configured SMTP server, guaranteeing 100% of your allocated quota goes directly to client engagement.
