## What FormMail Hub does

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Submission alerts" icon="email">
		Email you and your team every time someone submits your form. Works on the Free plan without any setup beyond choosing recipients.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Auto-replies and rules" icon="setting">
		Send your own email templates to the respondent or to different team members, depending on the answers given.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Campaigns from Google Sheets™" icon="document">
		Filter the people in your responses sheet and send them a bulk email from the FormMail Hub Sheets add-on.
	</Card>
	<Card title="QR tickets and check-in" icon="approve-check">
		Email each respondent a QR ticket, then check people in and out with a phone or a link, without staff accounts or passwords.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
