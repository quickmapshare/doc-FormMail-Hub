## 1. General & Email Automation FAQ

### Q: How do I send an email notification every time a new form submission is received?
**A:** This is the simplest and most essential feature for Form Admins. 
1. Access your FormMail Hub dashboard via the add-on sidebar (**"Go Beyond"** in Google Forms™ / **"Open Dashboard"** in Google Sheets™).
2. Navigate to **System Notifications**.
3. Toggle notifications **ON**, select yourself (Admin) and any desired Team Members to receive alerts, and click **Save**.
4. Every new submission will instantly trigger an internal email notification sent via built-in System SMTP to the designated recipients without consuming your personal email quota.

### Q: How can I route submissions to different team members based on specific response conditions?
**A:** 
1. Configure and test your **Custom SMTP** provider under **Settings > SMTP Config** (unlocks the *Templates*, *Rules*, *Campaigns*, and *Analytics* tabs).
2. Create custom Email Templates under **Email Templates**.
3. Go to **Email Rules** > **Conditional Rules** and configure multi-condition routing rules based on the respondent's form answers (e.g., Department equals "Support" or Budget > 5000).
4. Emails will be dynamically dispatched under your custom sender domain and transport.

### Q: How do I send custom confirmation emails and instructions directly to the form respondent?
**A:** 
1. Create a custom Email Template containing your instructions, brand styling, and dynamic tags (such as `{First Name}`, `{Form title}`, or `{All fields}`).
2. Create an **Auto-responder Rule** triggered on form submission.
3. Set the target recipient field to the dynamic email tag submitted by the respondent (e.g., `{Email}`).
4. When respondents complete the form, FormMail Hub immediately sends them their customized confirmation email.

### Q: Can I set a delay for emails triggered by form submission rules?
**A:** **Yes.** When setting up Auto-responder or conditional routing rules, you can configure an optional delivery delay. Admins can specify custom delay durations in **seconds**, **minutes**, **hours**, or **days** to schedule timed follow-up emails automatically. The cloud platform engine buffers and dispatches the email once the specified duration elapses.

## 2. Campaigns & Google Sheets Management Client

### Q: Can I proactively send bulk email notifications or campaigns to all form respondents?
**A:** **Yes.** Bulk sending (**Campaigns**) is launched exclusively via the **FormMail Hub Google Sheets add-on** while your Google Sheet is open.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                           CAMPAIGN CREATION & DISPATCH FLOW                       |
| 1. Admin creates a campaign template in FormMail Hub                              |
| 2. Admin configures filtering rules to target specific respondents in Sheet       |
| 3. Admin enables the rule as an active campaign                                   |
| 4. In Sheets add-on on responses sheet, Admin switches to Campaign view           |
| 5. Admin selects the synchronized campaign name                                   |
| 6. Admin clicks 'Dispatch' to execute bulk email dispatch via cloud platform      |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Q: Why is bulk sending only available in the Google Sheets add-on?
**A:** Google Sheets acts as the full-featured management client and data ledger for your respondent records. Because a single Google Spreadsheet can host multiple Form Responses tabs, connecting via the Sheets add-on uses only **1 connection slot** while scaling across multiple forms, providing the interface to filter rows and dispatch bulk campaigns efficiently.

## 3. QR Code Ticketing & Attendance Check-in FAQ

### Q: Is the QR Code and Link Check-in feature easy to set up and operate?
**A:** **Yes.** Setup is straightforward and requires no complex hardware or mobile app installations:
1. **Dispatch Authorization Links:** Send personalized scanner links (`{Check-in Scanner}`, `{Check-out Scanner}`, or `{Full Scanner}`) to staff or respondents to activate 7-day scanner permissions directly on their web browser.
2. **Include Passes in Confirmation Emails:** Include a `{QR Code}` (visual QR code + embedded link) or direct clickable `{Verify Link}` in the respondent's confirmation email.
3. **Execute Verification:** Upon arrival, staff scan the QR code with their mobile camera, or respondents/staff click the direct `{Verify Link}` on an authorized device. Real-time confirmation receipts and audit logs are automatically emailed to the respondent, Form Admin, and designated team members.

### Q: What are the main practical applications for the Check-in system?
**A:** The Check-in feature can be applied across several operational workflows:
- **Event ticketing and entry access control:** Issuing digital entry passes and scanning tickets at entry gates.
- **Workplace and academic classroom attendance tracking:** Tracking employee shift clock-ins or student lecture attendance.
- **Office PC Kiosk Attendance:** Allowing employees to verify daily check-in and check-out directly from their office desktop browser without physical scanners.
- **Equipment, asset, or library book loan and return management:** Recording asset checkout and checkin status transitions.

### Q: Can check-in be performed without a camera scanner?
**A:** **Yes.** Both attendees and staff can perform verification without a camera by clicking the direct manual verification link (`{Verify Link}`) rendered in their notification or confirmation emails on any authorized browser.

### Q: How does the system prevent duplicate check-ins?
**A:** When an authorized user or staff member accesses the verification link, the platform verifies permissions and updates the ticket's explicit state (`IN` or `OUT`). If a ticket status is already `IN`, duplicate check-in attempts are rejected with a conflict alert on screen. Every state change extends the record's retention to **30 days** without deleting data.

### Q: How do automated dual real-time attendance receipts work?
**A:** Upon every successful Check-in or Check-out event, the engine dispatches two independent, real-time email notifications:
1. **Attendee Attendance Email:** Dispatched to the ticket holder confirming their updated status (`Checked In` or `Checked Out`), Event Name, Reference ID, Location (if applicable), and UTC timestamp.
2. **Staff/Admin Audit Log Email:** Dispatched directly to the operator/user and **CC'd to the Form Owner** recording an audit trail containing attendee identity, ticket reference code, operator identity, and scan timestamp.

## 4. Analytics, Daily Reports & Subscription Management

### Q: What performance analytics and reports does FormMail Hub provide?
**A:** FormMail Hub tracks daily and cumulative performance stats for every form, categorized into three distinct execution modes:
- `system`: Automated operational alerts, daily reports, and SMTP connection error alerts.
- `automation`: Submission-triggered auto-responders and rule-based emails.
- `campaign`: Proactive bulk emails dispatched from the Google Sheets add-on.

Additionally, an automated **Daily Summary Report** is emailed via System SMTP to the Form Admin at **00:00 (according to each form's configured timezone)** for each active Form, summarizing the previous day's metrics.

### Q: How can Form Admins and respondents manage email preferences and prevent unwanted emails?
**A:** FormMail Hub provides precise, multi-tiered subscription control across 3 granular scopes:
- **Form-Specific Respondent Unsubscribe:** Custom email templates can include the `{Unsubscribe link}` tag. Clicking this link allows respondents to unsubscribe strictly from future emails related to that specific Form.
- **Form-Specific System Daily Report Unsubscribe:** Daily summary report emails sent to Admins include an unsubscribe link that stops daily reports strictly for that specific Form, keeping daily summary reports active for other forms.
- **User-Level SMTP Error Alert Unsubscribe:** SMTP error alert emails sent to Admins include an unsubscribe link scoped to the user account level, stopping SMTP failure alerts across all forms managed under that account.

## 5. SMTP Transport & Security FAQ

### Q: Why are the Templates, Rules, Campaigns, and Analytics tabs locked in the sidebar?
**A:** FormMail Hub requires custom SMTP for customer-facing emails. Admins **MUST** configure and test custom SMTP settings under **Settings > SMTP Config** before unlocking Templates, Rules, Campaigns, or Analytics. Until custom SMTP is verified with a successful test connection, these tabs remain strictly locked.

### Q: Can I use built-in System SMTP to send custom auto-responders to respondents?
**A:** **No.** Built-in System SMTP is strictly restricted to internal administrative alerts (submission notifications, daily summary reports, and SMTP connection error alerts) sent to the Form Admin and selected Team Members. Custom auto-responders and campaign broadcasts must route through verified Custom SMTP.

### Q: Does FormMail Hub support webhooks, Zapier, or third-party CRM integrations?
**A:** **No.** FormMail Hub operates strictly as an email receiving, processing, dispatching, and QR attendance tracking engine. It **DOES NOT** support webhooks, HTTP POST forwarding, external API calls, or third-party integrations (such as Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Zapier, or external CRMs).
