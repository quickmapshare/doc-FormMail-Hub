## Getting started

### How do I get an email every time someone submits my form?
Open **Submission Notifications**, turn notifications on, tick the recipients and click **Save Configuration**. No SMTP is needed for these alerts. See [Get started](/guides/formmailhub/).

### How do I send a confirmation email to the person who filled in the form?
[Connect your SMTP](/guides/smtp-setup/), [create a template](/guides/template-customization/), then [create a rule](/guides/conditional-logic/) that sends it to the respondent's email field.

### Why are Email Templates, Manage Rules, Campaigns and Analytics unavailable?
They unlock after you connect your SMTP and the test email succeeds. Connecting SMTP is free on every plan.

### Why can't another editor of my form change its settings?
The first editor who connects a form becomes its admin, and only the admin can change it. Other people can be added as [team members](/guides/team-management/) to receive emails.

## Sending

### Can the respondent's email be sent from FormMail Hub's own address?
No. FormMail Hub's own mail server only sends submission alerts, daily reports and error alerts to you and your team. Emails to respondents go through your SMTP so they come from your address.

### Can I send different emails depending on the answers?
Yes. Create one rule per case, each with its own conditions and template. See [Rules and conditions](/guides/conditional-logic/).

### Can I delay an email or send it at a fixed time?
Yes. A rule can wait seconds, minutes, hours or days, or send at a fixed date and time in the form's time zone.

### How do I email all, or some, respondents at once?
Use a [campaign](/guides/email-campaigns/), started from the FormMail Hub add-on in Google Sheets.

### Does FormMail Hub support webhooks, Slack, Zapier or other integrations?
No. FormMail Hub sends emails and handles QR check-in. It does not forward submissions to webhooks or other apps.

## QR check-in

### Do staff need an account or an app to scan tickets?
No. Staff open a scanner link once in their phone's browser, then scan with the camera. See [QR event tickets](/tutorials/qr-event-ticketing/).

### Can people check in without a camera?
Yes. Every ticket also has a link (`{Verify Link}`) that opens the same check-in page.

### Can a guest be checked in twice?
No. A second check-in is refused until the guest has been checked out.

### What else can check-in be used for?
Office or class attendance, and lending equipment or books. See [Employee attendance](/tutorials/office-pc-kiosk-attendance/).

## Plans

### What is the difference between Free and Pro?
All features are on both plans. Pro ($5/month or $39/year) removes the daily email limits, the template and rule limits, and the FormMail Hub footer. See [Plans & pricing](/guides/plans-and-pricing/).

### What happens when I cancel Pro?
You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. Payments are not refunded.

## Privacy

### Can people stop receiving emails?
Yes. Add `{Unsubscribe link}` to your templates. It stops future emails from that form to that person.

### Where can I read the privacy policy?
See [Security and privacy](/reference/security-privacy-compliance/), the [Privacy Policy](https://formmail.vietutd.com/privacy-policy) and the [Terms of Service](https://formmail.vietutd.com/terms-of-service).
