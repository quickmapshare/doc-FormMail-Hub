## 1. System Ecosystem Overview

FormMail Hub operates as an integrated multi-addon suite across Google Workspace. Rather than relying on a single entry point, the ecosystem allows users to initiate form automation directly from three targeted Google Forms™ add-ons or manage advanced email campaigns, lead databases, and settings from the core Google Sheets™ add-on.

### Core Application vs. Entry Add-ons
- **Core Application ("FormMail Hub" Google Sheets Add-on):** The main application. It processes form submit events recorded in the connected spreadsheet, manages dynamic variables, hosts the template engine, connects custom SMTP servers, manages team rosters, and executes bulk email broadcast campaigns.
- **Google Forms Entry Add-ons:** Three Google Forms add-ons connect directly to FormMail Hub to simplify specialized workflow setup inside the form builder:
  1. **Form Confirmation Emails:** Focused on configuring immediate auto-responders sent to form submitters.
  2. **Form to Email:** Focused on administrative and team notifications, routing form responses instantly to designated internal email addresses.
  3. **Form Notifications SMTP:** Specialized for connecting high-volume custom SMTP providers directly within Google Forms.

## 2. Roles & Permissions Architecture

FormMail Hub enforces strict role boundaries across both Google Forms and Google Sheets environments:

### Primary Connector / Admin Role
Only the **first user among the form's editors who connects that form to FormMail Hub** is granted exclusive rights to configure settings, templates, and triggers for that form. Co-editors on the Google Form or backing Google Sheet cannot modify or overwrite these settings unless administrative permissions are transferred by the primary connector.

### Team Member Logic
Team members are **ONLY** added so the Admin can select who receives specific email notifications upon form submission. Team members **DO NOT** have permission to edit, configure settings, or modify forms/campaigns.

## 4. Campaign Feature Logic & Constraints

### Why Campaigns are Strictly Google Sheets-Bound
The **Campaign (Bulk Sending) feature** is **ONLY** available when launched from the **FormMail Hub Google Sheets add-on**. It is intentionally disabled when accessing the dashboard via any of the three Google Forms entry add-ons.

### Technical Architectural Reasons:
1. **Data Source Requirement:** A bulk broadcast email dispatch requires structured contact records, field parameters, and historical response rows to construct the target audience. Google Forms API events only pass isolated runtime objects for *current single submissions*; they do not host persistent tabular contact databases.
2. **Spreadsheet Row Indexing:** FormMail Hub leverages the backing Google Sheet as its contact list and opt-out state storage. List segmentation, date filtering, dynamic custom column tags, and contact suppression verification rely on reading spreadsheet cell vectors.
3. **Execution Context & Quotas:** Google Apps Script execution limits for Google Forms triggers are stricter (30-second context limits). Google Sheets-bound script executions leverage longer batch background processing windows essential for iterating over contact lists.

## 5. Technical Data Flow & Execution Pipelines

### Real-time Form Submission Pipeline
When a user fills out a connected Google Form, execution flows as follows:

1. **Event Capture:** Google Form fires `onFormSubmit` event trigger.
2. **Payload Extraction:** FormMail Hub extracts submission fields, system timestamp, and submitter email.
3. **Sheet Synchronization:** Response payload is written to the connected Google Sheet.
4. **Rule Evaluation:** FormMail Hub evaluates active conditional logic rules defined by the Primary Connector (Admin).
5. **Template Rendering:** Dynamic tags (`{First Name}`, `{Form Summary}`, etc.) are replaced with parsed response values.
6. **SMTP Routing:** Email is dispatched via the configured route:
   - **Internal Alerts:** Dedicated system relay (0% personal quota used).
   - **Respondent Auto-responder:** Configured Custom SMTP (Amazon SES, SendGrid, Gmail App Password).
7. **Audit Logging:** Status (`DELIVERED`, `BOUNCED_HARD`, `FAILED_SMTP_AUTH`) is written to the audit log.

### Bulk Campaign Pipeline (Google Sheets Core Only)
1. **Audience Filtering:** Admin defines segment filters (e.g., `Column C = 'Premium'` AND `Opt-In Status != Suppressed`).
2. **Subscriber Verification:** The Campaign Engine queries spreadsheet rows and cross-references the suppression registry.
3. **Batch Queueing:** Dispatch jobs are batched into chunks to comply with provider rate limits.
4. **Asynchronous Send:** Messages are handed off to the Admin's Custom SMTP server.
5. **State Update:** Spreadsheet metadata and campaign analytics are updated in real time.

## 6. Migration & Interoperability

Users who install any of the entry-point Google Forms add-ons (`Form Confirmation Emails`, `Form to Email`, or `Form Notifications SMTP`) can transition to the core Google Sheets experience at any time without losing settings:

1. Ensure the Google Form is linked to a target Google Sheet (**Google Forms > Responses > Link to Sheets**).
2. Install and launch **FormMail Hub** from the target Google Sheet.
3. The core add-on automatically detects existing form rules, templates, and SMTP credentials established by the Primary Connector, granting full access to list indexing and the **Broadcast Campaign Engine**.
