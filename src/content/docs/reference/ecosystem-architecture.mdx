---
title: How the FormMail Hub add-ons fit together
description: Which Google Forms and Google Sheets add-ons connect to FormMail Hub, what each one is for, and where emails are actually sent from.
---

FormMail Hub is an online service with a web dashboard. Several Google add-ons connect your forms to it. They pass each new submission to FormMail Hub, which then sends the emails.

## The add-ons

| Add-on | Installed in | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **FormMail Hub** | Google Sheets™ | Managing several forms from one spreadsheet, and sending [campaigns](/guides/email-campaigns/) |
| **Form Confirmation Emails** | Google Forms™ | Connecting a single form |
| **Form to Email** | Google Forms™ | Connecting a single form |
| **Form Notifications SMTP** | Google Forms™ | Connecting a single form |

All of them open the same dashboard, with the same templates, rules, team members and plan.

A spreadsheet that collects responses from several forms can be managed with one Google Sheets add-on connection. If you manage many forms, the Google Sheets add-on is the more convenient choice.

## Where emails are sent from

| Email | Sent through |
| --- | --- |
| Submission alerts, daily reports, error alerts to you and your team | FormMail Hub's own mail server |
| Templates, rule emails, auto-replies and campaigns | Your [SMTP account](/guides/smtp-setup/) |
| Check-in and check-out receipts | Your SMTP account |

Submissions are queued on FormMail Hub's servers before emails are sent.
