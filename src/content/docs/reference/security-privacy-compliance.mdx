This page explains in plain terms what FormMail Hub stores and how it protects it. The legal terms are in the [Privacy Policy](https://formmail.vietutd.com/privacy-policy) and [Terms of Service](https://formmail.vietutd.com/terms-of-service).

## What is stored

- **Your form answers** stay in your Google Form and responses spreadsheet. FormMail Hub reads each submission to send the emails you configured.
- **Your settings** (templates, rules, team members and campaign settings) are stored by FormMail Hub so it can send emails on your behalf.
- **Your SMTP password or API key** is encrypted (AES-256) before it is saved, and is only used to send your emails.
- **Statistics**: daily counts of emails sent, opened and failed for each form.
- **Check-in records**: ticket status, time and the staff member who scanned. Each record is kept for 30 days after its last change.
- **Unsubscribe requests**, so that people who opted out are not emailed again.

## Access control

- Only the first editor who connects a form (its admin) can change its FormMail Hub settings. See [Team members](/guides/team-management/).
- Scanner and check-in links are digitally signed, so they cannot be altered or guessed. A scanner link authorizes one browser for 7 days.
- An employee's self check-in link only works for their own record.

## Unsubscribing

| Who | Link | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Respondents | `{Unsubscribe link}` in your templates | No more emails from that form to that address |
| Form admin | Link in the daily report | No more daily reports for that form |
| Form admin | Link in SMTP error alerts | No more SMTP error alerts, for all forms |

Add `{Unsubscribe link}` to any email you send to respondents, and especially to campaigns. Depending on your country, the law may require it.

## Your responsibilities

You decide who receives your emails and what they contain. Only email people who gave you their address, and follow the rules of your SMTP provider.
