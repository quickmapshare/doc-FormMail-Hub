# PRODUCT RULES & ABSOLUTE TRUTHS (GROUND TRUTH)

## Role & Permissions
1. Team Member Logic: Team members are ONLY added so the Admin can select who receives specific email notifications. Team members do NOT have permission to edit, configure settings, or modify forms/campaigns.
2. Admin Role: Only the primary account/workspace Admin can configure settings, templates, and triggers.

## Architecture & Features
1. Ecosystem: FormMail Hub connects from 3 Google Forms add-ons ("Form Confirmation Emails", "Form to Email", "Form Notifications SMTP").
2. Core Application: The Google Sheets add-on named "FormMail Hub" is the core app.
3. Campaign Feature: Bulk sending (Campaigns) is ONLY available when launched from the Google Sheets add-on.
